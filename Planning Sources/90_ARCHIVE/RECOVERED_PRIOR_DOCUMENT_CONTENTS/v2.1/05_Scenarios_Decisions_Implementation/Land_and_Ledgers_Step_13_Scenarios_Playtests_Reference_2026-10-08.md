## I. Global scenario doctrine
1. Objectives read real events and authoritative economic/physical state; objectives may not generate people, property, passengers, cargo, credit or business activity needed for their own completion (Canon XXXI p.7).
2. Each predicate is explicitly `Event`, `Current`, `Maintained`, or `Cumulative`, with a dated evidence reference and scenario-qualified geographic scope.
3. Completion of an event milestone normally survives later disposal of an enabling asset; maintained or current ownership qualifications reevaluate.
4. Ownership qualification explicitly distinguishes sole, joint, controlling, minority and genuinely independent NPC state.
5. Scenario equity uses authored asset/liability inclusion, valuation basis and starting-asset policy; debt proceeds do not create equity.
6. Scenario failure does not automatically equal commercial bankruptcy or destruction of the persistent world (Canon XXXI p.114).
7. Role constraints in a deliberate scenario may restrict the player’s labor, but do not change ordinary simulation rules for everyone else.
8. Keep text-playtest data, rules and executable Unity test results in separate source/evidence categories.
