## C. Previously covered or reaffirmed by governing Canon / prior design directions — 48

These entries are already within the scope of preserved doctrine or previously approved direction; exact source routes are in the crosswalk. This is a topic-level reconciliation, **not** evidence of exhaustive clause-level certification.

- **1.1 Realism and historical plausibility** — Ask what people in this period with this information, incentives and material constraints would actually do; favor causation over scripted gamification and research dated historical claims.
- **1.2 Independent world evolution** — People, business ownership, employees, property uses, leases, debt, buildings and opportunities must change through real processes over decades; startup data never resets live state.
- **1.3 Player-NPC economic parity** — NPCs share the player’s physical, economic, property, contract, labor, lending, investing and business-formation authorities, subject to their actual knowledge, resources and judgement.
- **1.4 No scripted success or protection** — Do not protect a business, impose market shares, or predetermine economic winners to satisfy a scenario; actors survive, adapt or fail through actual economics.
- **2.1 Permanent NPC business failure** — Any firm may close permanently when no viable lawful economic path remains; being the only local supplier is not immunity.
- **2.4 Independent lender underwriting** — Creditors judge borrower, collateral, income, obligations and credit risk using their legitimate evidence and legal authority; refusal, default and enforcement have causal consequences.
- **2.8 Seller financing** — A legitimate sale may combine cash with seller-carried promissory notes, interest, schedules, collateral and default remedies; record real lender and borrower rights.
- **2.9 Person persists after business failure** — Failure or dispossession must not delete the former owner or erase acquired skills, relationships, needs and options; they can seek work, change field, move or found another firm.
- **3.1 NPC business formation** — A Person with viable resources, opportunities, knowledge and access may establish a generic enterprise, lease space, acquire stock and hire labor.
- **3.2 Opportunity discovered from evidence** — NPCs notice actual shortages, prices, customer travel, weak service, unused assets, rentable space, labor supply, trade and cash opportunities; firms do not spawn from an arbitrary timer.
- **3.4 Periodic economic evaluation** — Persons, households and firms periodically consider cash, debt, essential needs, employment, consumption, savings, expansion, asset investment and business creation through their proper authorities.
- **4.1 Customer purchasing decisions** — Purchase quantity, supplier choice and loyalty derive from price, availability, quality, travel, reliability, relationships, needs and budget together.
- **4.3 Experience and supplier relationships** — Satisfactory quality, timely delivery, shortages, bad service and repeat interactions change relationship evidence and future buying preferences.
- **4.4 Causal competition** — Businesses can change price, capacity, sourcing, quality, reliability, services or customer relationships in response to genuine competitors; never assign fixed market shares.
- **5.1 Individual risk profiles** — Persistent but modifiable individual risk tolerance and preferences influence debt, opportunity, saving and expansion decisions.
- **5.3 Human imperfect judgment** — NPC forecasts and reactions may be mistaken, overconfident, fearful or biased according to actual evidence and character; they are not omniscient perfect optimizers.
- **6.5 Asset ownership versus access** — Buying, borrowing, renting, leasing, hiring an operator and commissioning a service create distinct ownership, custody, permission, cost and liability relationships.
- **6.6 Equipment rentals and lease terms** — Allow historically supported short- and long-term access to horses, wagons and tools through legitimate agreement terms instead of forcing ownership for temporary jobs.
- **7.1 Separate property and business title** — Track ownership of fee title, buildings, goods, equipment and business interests separately from leases, management, tenancy, occupancy and rights of use.
- **7.4 Enterprise value versus real estate** — An operating business value depends on assets, goodwill, current and prospective profits, workers, contracts, reputation and obligations; appraisals and property values remain distinguishable.
- **8.2 Stable Building identity** — Every Building gets a stable distinct identifier independent of occupant, use, owner or business identity.
- **8.4 Rooms as physical spaces** — Rooms/FunctionalSpaces have dimensions, position, entry/access, capacity and other physical attributes; template labels do not impose permanent usage restrictions.
- **8.7 Multiple households and uses per Building** — One Building may host multiple lawful households, separate residential units and independent businesses through distinct FunctionalSpace and occupancy/use relationships.
- **8.10 Runtime room and building changes** — After startup, ownership, room tenants, furnishing, business location, expansions, leases, renovation and construction all evolve through live state, unaffected by original template settings.
- **8.11 Property owner distinct from business tenants** — Landlords may lease rooms to many independent operators; owner-operated, mixed-use and multi-firm arrangements follow specific rights and usable spaces.
- **9.1 Physical freight lifecycle** — Procurement, assigned freight responsibility, negotiation, asset preparation, travel, loading, shipment, unloading, custody, acceptance and settlement are distinct events and costs.
- **9.2 Multiple transport choices** — Legitimate parties can contract for supplier delivery, buyer pickup, self-haul, third-party carrier, owned fleet, rental equipment or hired staff; intelligent defaults do not remove choice.
- **9.3 Self-haul uses real resources** — Self-haul avoids a carrier fee but consumes worker time, animals, vehicles, feed, journey time, maintenance and forgone opportunities.
- **9.4 Plausible NPC negotiations** — A Person may visit a service provider, ask an associate or use period handbills, notices and formal communication; awareness must spread by legitimate channels.
- **9.9 Arrival-driven walking** — Physical A-to-B walk tasks end on actual arrival along the route, not an arbitrary fixed timer.
- **9.10 Off-map regional journey state** — Use a departure point then an authoritative off-map journey with elapsed time, distance, inventory/cargo custody, equipment and resource consumption; do not teleport stock.
- **9.11 Real animal and vehicle assets** — Horse and wagon assets must retain owner, availability, condition, location and compatible use, not exist as a visual-only freight effect.
- **9.12 Multi-stop and pooled freight** — Support multi-stop collections and deliveries, backhauls, through-cargo, employee assignments, optional subcontract and pooled authorized vehicle use while preserving property and contractual ownership.
- **10.2 Persons retain careers and skills** — A Person outlasts a job or business, retaining memory, accumulated skills, professional opportunities and identity after moving or retraining.
- **10.3 Human labor constraints** — Labour depends on sleep, nutrition, fatigue, commuting, skills, overtime, mistakes, lateness, preference, wages, raises, bonuses and outside job offers, without excessive owner micro-management.
- **10.4 Non-overlapping task priorities** — Survival and personal needs, work and travel compete for finite actual Person time; infeasible parallel tasks wait for an available slot.
- **11.1 Household needs consume resources** — Households acquire and consume real goods to satisfy needs and those purchases reduce their resources and sellers' physical stocks.
- **11.2 Finite household purchasing power** — Households budget finite cash/income against debts, necessities, housing, product prices, quality and travel, rather than spending unlimited money.
- **11.3 Stock and custody precede consumption** — Bought items do not become locally consumable until genuinely received or transferred into the consuming Person or household's possession.
- **13.3 Temporal and spatial coherence** — Faster simulation must advance tasks, motion, consumption and distance in consistent time units without impossible movement or resource throughput.
- **14.4 Unity startup authoring versus live state** — Unity scene supplies initial physical setup, not continuing ownership, tenant, debt, inventory or work-state authority; economic simulation becomes controlling after start.
- **15.1 Inter-business physical transfers** — Owned businesses can request quantity, percentage, cadence and reserve-based inventory transfers, limited by real stock, storage, rights, schedule, labor and transport; internal prices may follow existing agreements.
- **15.2 Composable enterprise activities** — Extraction, craft, retail, transport and service depend on real methods and resources under the shared generic Business authority, not a hard industry-class gate.
- **15.3 Physical local stock and portfolio read models** — Inventory remains with physical custodian/location; portfolio dashboards show derived totals without creating centralized instantly spendable goods.
- **15.4 Foreclosure and property security** — Debt default, collateral sale, insolvency, security interests and foreclosure follow time, contract, evidence and legal processes; destroying collateral does not erase creditor claims.
- **15.5 Every actor is an economic participant** — Persons, households, firms and communities make legitimate economic and social choices; demographic representation must rely on sourced, contextual mechanisms instead of stereotypes or generic hostility.
- **15.6 Delegated manager authority** — Managers handle routine procurement, staffing, service and production without owner physical presence while retaining explicit authorization for mortgages, major loans, transfers of ownership and other material decisions.
- **15.7 Causal settlement transformation** — Town growth responds to households, transport, buildings, land, demand, industry and institutions through real-world processes; avoid population thresholds or scripted building unlocks.

## D. Newly clarified or newly specified user mechanics — 40

- **1.5 Meaningful choices** — Offer distinct interactions only if they meaningfully differ in money, obligations, rights, risk, access, time, relationships or legality; avoid decorative option overload and verbose explanations.
- **2.2 Gradual financial distress** — Financial difficulties unfold through cash strain, attempts to adapt, renegotiation, borrowing, layoffs, equipment or real-estate sales, restructuring and possibly shutdown rather than immediate automatic deletion.
- **2.3 Borrowing into distress** — Some people double down and borrow against hoped-for recovery while others liquidate early; flawed judgement and distinct personalities affect the path until lenders refuse.
- **2.5 Distress asset liquidation** — An owner can sell actual transferable inventory, horses, wagons, equipment, buildings, land or business interests to raise cash, potentially at distressed prices; liens, leases and other rights survive.
- **2.6 Distressed business acquisitions** — A struggling owner can approach known people for a loan, new investment, or purchase discussion, and buyers can investigate commercial viability, assets, relationships and vertical integration without scripted offers.
- **2.7 Business due diligence and imperfect records** — Prospective buyers can seek books, cash, revenues, expenses, inventory, equipment, debt, obligations, tenure, contracts and prospects through lawful access; information quality, incomplete books and access limits affect confidence and terms.
- **2.8 Seller financing** — A legitimate sale may combine cash with seller-carried promissory notes, interest, schedules, collateral and default remedies; record real lender and borrower rights.
- **2.10 Flight and extreme distress** — Severe distress may cause flight from creditors or deprivation, including food insecurity; disappearance does not cancel debt, title, liens, custodial obligations or jurisdictional enforcement.
- **3.3 Skill changes outcomes, not generic access permissions** — A blacksmith needs substantial skill for safe, timely high-quality output; an untrained operator cannot instantly produce masterwork, but Canon XXXI still permits physically feasible ordinary attempts; enterprise owners may instead hire skilled workers.
- **3.4 Periodic economic evaluation** — Persons, households and firms periodically consider cash, debt, essential needs, employment, consumption, savings, expansion, asset investment and business creation through their proper authorities.
- **5.1 Individual risk profiles** — Persistent but modifiable individual risk tolerance and preferences influence debt, opportunity, saving and expansion decisions.
- **5.2 Personality interacts with context** — Even a risk-seeker evaluates actual cash, dependants, debt, collateral, experience and available opportunities; a cautious person may still accept well-supported risk.
- **5.3 Human imperfect judgment** — NPC forecasts and reactions may be mistaken, overconfident, fearful or biased according to actual evidence and character; they are not omniscient perfect optimizers.
- **6.1 Unsolicited asset offers** — A player or NPC may approach an identified owner with an offer on an asset not publicly listed; actual ownership, authority, willingness and counteroffer determine whether it proceeds.
- **6.2 Asset utilization and reservation value** — Productive utilization, expected income loss and replacement difficulty increase the cost of parting with a horse, wagon or other equipment.
- **6.4 Cash position modifies seller willingness** — Financially secure owners usually need more reason to sell useful assets, while overleveraged owners may liquidate underused or even productive assets; combine utilization, debt, cash, risk and market availability.
- **6.6 Equipment rentals and lease terms** — Allow historically supported short- and long-term access to horses, wagons and tools through legitimate agreement terms instead of forcing ownership for temporary jobs.
- **6.7 Renter damage and disputes** — Renter and owner allocation of loss, maintenance, damage, wear and repairs follows negotiated agreement, evidence and dated law; the renter may carry liability where contracted.
- **7.2 Business purchase excludes third-party property** — Acquiring an operating Bakery does not convey the landlord's building or land; any real estate transfers only if the actual owner agrees and can lawfully convey it.
- **7.3 Negotiated acquisition scope** — Negotiated transactions may transfer some inventory, tools, whole enterprise, partial interest, owned buildings, land and selected liabilities; verify ownership, approvals, liens and contract assignment.
- **7.4 Enterprise value versus real estate** — An operating business value depends on assets, goodwill, current and prospective profits, workers, contracts, reputation and obligations; appraisals and property values remain distinguishable.
- **8.1 Editor-authored initial settlement** — Build the initial town as Unity-authored physical plots, roads, structures and seed assignments; after initialization the simulation owns runtime transitions.
- **8.2 Stable Building identity** — Every Building gets a stable distinct identifier independent of occupant, use, owner or business identity.
- **8.3 Inspector layout presets** — A Unity Inspector layout selection can initialize a physical room configuration such as five rooms but must never act as an immutable business/residential class.
- **8.4 Rooms as physical spaces** — Rooms/FunctionalSpaces have dimensions, position, entry/access, capacity and other physical attributes; template labels do not impose permanent usage restrictions.
- **8.5 Outfitting enables activity** — Actual beds, counters, oven, ventilation, furnishings and associated labor/rights determine whether a space supports lodging, baking, storage or another activity; unfurnished rooms are not functional bedrooms by default.
- **8.6 Equipment footprint and safety** — Station equipment must physically fit with access, work area and required conditions; capacity, ventilation, fire exposure and dated law constrain activity without arbitrary RoomType permissions.
- **8.7 Multiple households and uses per Building** — One Building may host multiple lawful households, separate residential units and independent businesses through distinct FunctionalSpace and occupancy/use relationships.
- **8.8 Starting assignment modes** — Editor starting-use choices are Automatic, Assigned and Vacant; each seeds an initial occupancy relationship and cannot control future live occupancy.
- **8.9 Starting identity selections** — Initial Assigned choices reference the actual configured starting Business or Household identities and reject incompatible exclusive double assignments.
- **9.4 Plausible NPC negotiations** — A Person may visit a service provider, ask an associate or use period handbills, notices and formal communication; awareness must spread by legitimate channels.
- **9.5 Verbal freight agreements** — Ordinary freight agreements may be oral; the simulation records principals, terms, cargo, price, obligations and evidence even if no written instrument exists.
- **9.7 Mileage-based freight quotes** — Freight price may include base/dispatch fee plus rate per expressly defined chargeable distance; terms must specify loaded, empty, one-way, return and mileage responsibility.
- **9.9 Arrival-driven walking** — Physical A-to-B walk tasks end on actual arrival along the route, not an arbitrary fixed timer.
- **9.10 Off-map regional journey state** — Use a departure point then an authoritative off-map journey with elapsed time, distance, inventory/cargo custody, equipment and resource consumption; do not teleport stock.
- **10.1 Young workers exist** — Younger people may do historically supported work/apprenticeship within actual ability, applicable law and availability; remove an unnecessary legacy YoungWorker category only where code audit confirms duplication.
- **11.4 Autonomous circular economy** — Real household needs drive purchases, retail replenishment, procurement, freight, production, wages and new customer opportunities as interconnected loops.
- **12.1 Death leaves estate and claims** — Death does not erase ownership or assets; estates, heirs, managers and creditors continue through historically applicable succession and debt rules.
- **12.2 Heirs do not inherit work skills** — An heir can inherit property or business ownership without operational skills and may appoint employees, lease or sell it under governing law; no universal spouse-inherits-everything rule.

## F. Conflicts or boundaries that should **not** be silently settled

1. **Skill gating versus universal ordinary attemptability** — 3.3 should affect competence, quality, time, safety and specialist prerequisites; Canon XXXI explicitly forbids general occupation gates. Only a more restrictive new skill gate would require owner approval.
2. **New inheritance confirmations versus pending proposal #115** — 12.1–12.2 confirm estate continuity, not blanket approval of every piece of #115 or its detailed legal implementation.
3. **Realism-mode global knowledge** — Nov 2 note permits instant **owned-business reporting convenience** in default mode, not omniscience of private NPC records. Historical mode would impose knowledge latency. Exact policy remains open.
4. **Provisional tariff and timing figures** — 9.6, 9.8, 13.2 and 8.12 cannot become universal values without separate approval and testing.
5. **Dated historical law** — Estate division, lease liability, adolescent labor and debt remedies require geographic and temporal historical legal research; do not invent universal laws.
6. **Asset offers versus legal transferability** — Soliciting a sale must not grant rights to buy restricted or third-party property.

**There is no need to halt the documentation release for these boundary questions. They are precisely documented and not silently answered.**
