## Purpose and constitutional boundary

A single legally unrestricted `LandForSale=true`, `Bank=true`, `LoanType=true` or `TownLicense=true` flag would falsify the period. Federal, territorial/state, county and municipal law may govern different aspects of the same Business. Treat legal regimes as dated authoring with provenance, not blanket progression unlocks. Physical opportunity, legal authority and actual prosecution/enforcement remain separate causal layers; historically possible unlawful action must not silently transform into consequence-free action.

## Hierarchy of evidence

1. **Verified direct statutory record / official government account:** grounds general federal timing or identified legal rights but may not establish local implementation details.
2. **Canon preserved historical finding:** identifies a potentially important legal constraint but does not establish exact full statutory wording unless independently sourced.
3. **Research hold:** exact date, exception, county, legal party, procedure, retroactivity or applicability is unresolved; do not hard-code.

## Legal authoring schema

`LegalRegimeRule`: jurisdiction geography and hierarchy; effective-from/to; linked statute/treaty/charter/decision; affected actor type and instrument; lawful/unlawful/prohibited distinction; prerequisite steps; remedy/enforcement actor; known exceptions; confidence and source; migration/continuity rules for existing claims.

`PropertyInterest`: parcel/site or movable asset; claimant/holder; full title, lease, right-of-way, easement, entry, trust restriction, mortgage or other real interest; effective date; document/notice/recording evidence; possession/custody; relative priority and extinguishment/transfer procedure.

## Main legal findings

### LAW-FED-1862-HOMESTEAD — Federal Homestead Act

**Effective from:** 1862-05-20. **Jurisdiction:** United States, surveyed eligible public land. **Domain:** Land entry/claims.

**Status:** VERIFIED FEDERAL FRAME. **Evidence:** Federal statute; https://www.archives.gov/milestone-documents/homestead-act.

**Historical rule / research finding:** Allows eligible claimants to enter surveyed eligible public land and seek patent after residence/improvement/proof; criteria and later amendments must be checked.

**Simulation authority:** Entry, residence, improvements, proof, patent, transfer restrictions.

**Guardrail:** Not applicable to reserved/trust land or private parcels solely because empty.

### LAW-FED-1863-BANK — National Currency Act

### LAW-TREATY-1868-LARAMIE — 1868 Treaty of Fort Laramie

**Effective from:** 1868. **Jurisdiction:** Lakota and other signatory nations; Great Sioux Reservation and related lands. **Domain:** Treaty and sovereign land rights.

**Status:** VERIFIED BROAD RIGHTS; GIS HOLD. **Evidence:** National Park Service; https://www.nps.gov/places/1868-treaty-field-area.htm.

**Simulation authority:** Geographic legal status, treaty and reservation rights; not ordinary sale listing.

### LAW-FED-1878-BANKRUPTCY — Federal bankruptcy repeal and gap

**Effective from:** 1878. **Jurisdiction:** United States. **Domain:** Insolvency legal regime.

**Status:** VERIFIED FEDERAL TIMELINE. **Evidence:** Federal Judicial Center / US Courts; https://www.rib.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/FJC%20Bankruptcy%20Timeline.pdf.

**Guardrail:** No modern bankruptcy reset; creditors remain capable of ordinary remedies.

**Effective from:** 1902-06-17. **Jurisdiction:** Western states and territories with eligible projects. **Domain:** Federal land/water institutions.

## Acceptance tests

- Filed homestead and patented fee-simple land may have different transfer/collateral powers; unentered reserve/treaty land must not appear in general land offers.
- A Black Hills parcel's treaty status must be resolved from region/date/rights, not a `West=true` boolean.
- 1889 statehood changes the applicable legal rule set while existing debts, titles and continuing economic entities persist.
- National bank regulatory limits must not be inherited by a private individual lender simply because both loan money.
- 1898 bankruptcy availability does not create a modern Chapter 11 or cancel existing collateral.
- State/territorial finance rules may differ from federal bankruptcy and corporation powers, with conflicting cases surfaced for research.
- The original historical event toggle must not suppress legal dates or Indigenous rights.
