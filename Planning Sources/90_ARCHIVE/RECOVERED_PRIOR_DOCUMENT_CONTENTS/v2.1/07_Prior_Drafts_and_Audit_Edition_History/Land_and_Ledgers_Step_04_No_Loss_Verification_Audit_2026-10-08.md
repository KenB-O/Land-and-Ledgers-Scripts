## Supersession resolutions

Current Canon governs the **1870–1905** normal Open Frontier horizon; older 1880-start references are archived scenario/historical context, not equal authority. Current Canon treats Business labels and occupations as descriptive, forbids capability flags as replacement business classes, allows novice ordinary production attempts subject to physical conditions and independent Person response, and requires physical settlement, not objective or dashboard magic. Specific legal institution powers and property rights remain genuine distinctions.

The four prior decision questions are resolved by **reading existing higher-priority rules**, not by adopting new mechanics: (1) possible versus lawful versus willing are distinct, including supported deliberate illegality; (2) finance office to deposit bank is an optional systemic route rather than a tier ladder; (3) EmploymentRelationship owns conventional employment but does not absorb family/independent/owner work into artificial wages; (4) 112 occupation profiles are a recommended scope/calibration catalog and not a hard class permission system.
