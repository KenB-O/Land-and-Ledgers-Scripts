# LAND & LEDGERS
## HISTORICAL TIMELINE, EVENTS AND ECONOMIC REGIMES
### RESEARCH REFERENCE — FIRST EDITION (STEP 08)

**Date:** 8 October 2026 | **Period:** 1870–1905 | **Authority:** Reference and authoring evidence only; Canon XXXI governs mechanics.

This catalog is deliberately a **dated, cited research record**, not a list of automatic economic modifiers. A nationwide crisis may create financial/counterparty strain while a rural Dakota node without relevant lender connections sees a delayed or small effect. Conversely, an independently generated drought or lender failure is still allowed when named historical event injection is disabled.

## I. Four noninterchangeable classes of historical entry

1. **Authored historical event (`EVT`)** — A researched named crisis, outbreak, or incident that may inject scoped causal inputs only when enabled by the scenario Historical Events setting.
2. **Historical period or regime (`REG`)** — A longer state of institutions, outside trade networks or development conditions; effects still require real local exposure, information and counterparties.
3. **Legal or availability boundary (`LAW`/`CTX`)** — A fixed date when legal jurisdiction, organizational powers or channel availability changes; these are not discretionary crisis cards and are not disabled merely by toggling Historical Events OFF.
4. **Campaign baseline (`HIST`)** — World setup envelope and authoring priors; dated evidence should constrain generation but must not force historical outcomes when player/NPC action changes the conditions.

## II. Event-evaluation grammar

`EventDefinition` (stable ID) -> date window and applicable region -> research/evidence check -> scenario authored toggle if applicable -> affected authoritative inputs (weather/route, finance/lender, crop/animal, information, person/household, law) -> ordinary tasks, agreement performance, prices and choices -> player-facing evidence and forecast.

**Never inject:** arbitrary `Revenue=-20%`, `Population+50`, immediate livestock disappearance, a depot because the calendar reached 1886, automatic bankruptcy upon panic, or an invisible forced settlement script.

**Event record minimum fields:** ID; title; valid period and geographic scope; class/toggle eligibility; historical source and research grade; local exposure conditions; causal input authorities; known uncertainty; save version; applied-event identity; reversible or irreversible actions; narrative/reporting access; scenario-specific overrides.

## V. Research holds and exclusions

- **Grasshopper and local crop crises:** An 1874–1877 episode is a promising historical regime candidate but requires direct Dakota time/place/source confirmation before implementing a fixed event ID with local crop multipliers.
- **Great Dakota Boom:** Treat settlement, railway approach, migration and land acquisition as processes through real agents; do not force every player settlement to grow on a historical aggregate boom schedule.
- **Railroad arrival:** Retain independent infrastructure and commercial service stages; Black Hills and Southeastern Dakota rail dates require corridor and corporate verification.
- **Indigenous treaty, reservation and land-law changes:** Land jurisdiction and rights require their own REF-02 legal-geography catalog; they are not generic optional 'disaster' toggles.
- **Conflicting mortality/loss percentages:** Ranch loss figures from different NPS cases apply to their described herds and ranges; never generalize a local 80% report to all ranches.
- **Post-horizon exclusions:** Postal Parcel Post (1913), the Federal Reserve System (1913) and Sears kit houses beginning in 1908 must not enter normal-period availability catalogs by mistake.

## VI. Acceptance fixtures

**PANIC-1873 OFF:** Authored national Panic inputs do not inject; an organic bank with real deposit-liquidity shortage can still fail.

**PANIC-1873 ON, isolated town:** Only connected lenders, railway finance and other real economic routes transmit relevant historical disturbance; unrelated local inventories are not automatically repriced.

**BLIZZARD-1888 geographic contrast:** Physical route and communication damage in a represented corridor may differ materially across Black Hills and eastern Dakota; do not stamp the same fatality, snow-depth or road-blocking profile everywhere.

**STATEHOOD-1889 event toggle OFF:** Dated state/legal geography remains in effect; existing debt/title/Persons persist without being rewritten.

**RFD-1896:** An isolated Dakota farm does not automatically receive a newly completed rural mail route simply because West Virginia began a federal experiment.

**RECLAMATION-1902:** Federal program exists, but unfinished local irrigation works do not produce water deliveries or create farm revenue until actual projects and water-service rights exist.

## VII. Evidence standard

Official government histories and period engineering records support specific factual dates or mechanisms. Geographic extrapolation is labeled. A reference date does not prove exact profitability, incidence, labor days or annual event magnitude in a procedurally generated town. Scenario-specific numeric calibration requires separate evidence.
