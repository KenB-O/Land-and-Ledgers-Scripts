## Authority-safe consequence
Because the full PDF originals remain governing, failure to independently certify a shorter rewrite does **not** discard the existing rules. Older active headings in the cumulative original PDFs are subordinate to their own explicit newer revision layers. Summaries remain search/navigation aids only.
