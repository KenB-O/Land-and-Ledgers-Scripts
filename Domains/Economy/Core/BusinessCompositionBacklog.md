# Business composition backlog

These concepts remain distinct in the permanent business architecture:

- Multiple operations within one business: one legal/economic `Business` identity owns several `BusinessOperation` or `CommercialCapability` records. Operations share the business ledger but retain capability-specific inputs, labor, and outputs.
- Operating group / enterprise: separate businesses remain separate legal and economic authorities while an `OperatingGroup` provides a management view across them. Grouping must not merge cash, ownership, debt, or profit.
- Consolidation / amalgamation: a separately authorized transaction creates one surviving business identity and records the predecessor identities, transferred assets, liabilities, contracts, and continuity evidence. It is not a management grouping or a synergy bonus.

Future work should add these authorities only when a concrete scenario or legal workflow requires them. Existing formation, premises, employment, inventory, logistics, and finance authorities should remain the source of truth until then.
