# THE FIRST VEIN — Playtest Finding VEIN-UX-012

Date: 2026-10-19; conceptual scenario time: 1877-12-16.

## Player feedback
The simulation is losing engagement because low-level sampling, title inquiries, vendor quotes, freight and payroll substeps trigger too many conversational turns. Historical and economic depth should remain in the simulation; it need not be exposed as constant approval prompts.

## Operating rule
- Players set strategy, investment budgets, hiring and termination policy, sale approvals and ownership constraints.
- Trusted managers handle routine work, sample testing, market contact, procurement and communications within their delegated authority; act concurrently.
- Advance 30-90 in-game days where feasible; stop only for material cash, safety, finance, staffing, ownership or sales decisions.
- Show short management summaries with true-to-ledger events, not every mundane sub-action; offer deeper audit views on demand.
- Preserve identity/asset custody, time/work assignments, lending security and historical legal realities behind the scenes.
- Contextual terminology tooltips per VEIN-UX-011.

## Player standing ownership and finance preferences
- 100% of Juniper No. 1 mining venture retained. Do not negotiate equity dilution in it.
- Aim for 100% mill company equity where achievable; no equity investor automatically.
- Purchase legally transferable full land ownership, not merely lease/claim if avoidable.
- Prefer low deposits and seller notes: a deed now with purchase-money mortgage collateral may satisfy land ownership (subject to foreclosure risk). Equipment can carry separate chattel security.
- Prefer an actual accessible mill site, lawful water and tailings agreements and sustainable ore feed.
- Don't represent a mill as profit-producing without reliable production/recovery and operating capital; make own processing accessible but not magically profitable.
- Mine operations Amos, freight Eli; player controls ore sales and staffing.

## UI implementation implications
Strategic 'Portfolio Orders' interface for multi-month delegation, capital plan, hiring policy, target outputs and exceptions. Alerts should consolidate related approvals. Financial and physical ledgers remain available through expandable audit panels.
