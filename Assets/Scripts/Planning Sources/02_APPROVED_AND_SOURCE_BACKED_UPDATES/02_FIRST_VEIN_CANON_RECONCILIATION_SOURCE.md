## Design requirements to reconcile into governing Canon

FV-CAN-001 **Strategic delegation without compulsory micromanagement.** The simulation may remain physically/economically detailed, but an owner may delegate routine operations to managers and advance long periods without repetitive authorizations. Delays, events and decisions emerge from real causes.

FV-CAN-002 **Player preference is policy, not hard-coded world law.** In THE FIRST VEIN the owner keeps 100% of Juniper No. 1 and prefers fully owned mill and purchased land; other ownership/lease/partnership methods remain possible if the player opts in. No equity dilution occurs as a default financing suggestion.

FV-CAN-003 **Owner control of exceptional risk.** Routine mine work, ordinary ore sale, limited procurement, scheduling and allowed collections are delegated; extraordinary loans, permanent hires, large equipment, new construction, title acquisition and ownership changes require authority compatible with owner standing policy.

FV-CAN-004 **Real financing and ownership.** Lower down payments, purchase-money security and seller notes are available through genuine willing counterparties, with actual collateral, maturity, debt service and enforcement. A filed mineral or millsite location is not automatically owned fee land.

FV-CAN-005 **No guaranteed mine profit.** Geological outcomes, assay uncertainty, grade, loss, labor, haulage, recovery, market terms, competition and risk control economics. Owning freight or a mill may produce real savings or added value, but never an abstract multiplier or guaranteed gold.

FV-CAN-006 **Physically conserved mineral flow.** Selected specimens, representative sampling, mined lots, assayed material, sold raw ore, custom toll milling, own recovered product, tailings and lost material remain distinguishable; no quest objective may invent product or count a raw-ore payment as owner recovered-and-sold gold.

FV-CAN-007 **Open commercial interactions within evidence limits.** The player and NPCs can seek contacts, referrals, supply, work and deals using legitimate networks and role-agnostic ordinary actions; search and structured proposals are valid default UX, not a mandatory LLM.

FV-CAN-008 **Evidence-based legal agreements.** Written and oral commitments can be performed/breached; proof, recordation, notice, priorities, remedies, disinterested witnesses, disputes, fixtures and improvements depend on law/jurisdiction and actual terms. No automatic invalidation because written evidence is absent.

FV-CAN-009 **Independent economic actors.** NPCs establish real enterprises, finance and trade with their own information and resources. Population results from real Persons/Households, not scenario target injection. Player-owned subsidiaries do not count as independent NPC firms.

FV-CAN-010 **Objective legibility.** Every scenario objective states its exact event, cumulative/current/maintained predicate, period, owner/revenue valuation basis, clock and exclusion cases; show progress without demanding ledger archaeology. THE FIRST VEIN has exactly seven goals, in separate scenario fixture.

FV-CAN-011 **Pacing and accessibility.** Permit optional 30–90 day progression through continuing activities if material exception decisions do not arise; show a short financial/work dashboard and plain-language definitions for assay, ore, title, tailings and debt.
